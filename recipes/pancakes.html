Peanut Butter and Banana Pancakes

Description

This recipe yields 12 4-inch pancakes and goes great with maple syrup and some strawberry or blueberry jam. You'll never need another pancake recipe after this!

Ingredients

  • 1 and 1/2 cups all-purpose flower
  • 2 tablespoons baking powder
  • 1 tablespoon white sugar
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 and 1/4 cups milk
  • 1/3 cup smooth peanut butter
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 tablespoon canola oil
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
